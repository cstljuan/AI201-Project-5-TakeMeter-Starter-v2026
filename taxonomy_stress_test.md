# Arqade taxonomy stress test

These eight posts are fictional boundary examples. They were generated to check the written rules before collecting the full dataset.

| # | Fictional post | Label | Why it fits |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | My game stutters each time I enter the city. I lowered shadows already. What should I check next? | `help_request` | The author asks for troubleshooting. |
| 2 | Fast travel is useless. | `unsupported_claim` | It makes an evaluative claim without a reason or example. |
| 3 | The crash log names frame 60 on every run, and the problem began after driver 555.85 was installed. | `supported_claim` | The post gives a checkable log detail and a time comparison. |
| 4 | Open Settings, choose Video, turn off ray tracing, then restart the game. | `guide_or_solution` | It gives actionable steps. |
| 5 | I think I found a fix: add `-dx11` to launch options. Does that solve this for everyone? | `help_request` | The direct request for validation controls the label, even though it includes a tentative method. |
| 6 | Route A has three checkpoints worth ten seconds each, so it finishes thirty seconds faster than Route B. | `supported_claim` | The conclusion is supported by explicit values. |
| 7 | Back up the save folder, disable cloud sync, and verify the game files before launching again. | `guide_or_solution` | It gives a sequence of recovery actions. |
| 8 | This boss is cheap because it's cheap. | `unsupported_claim` | Repeating the opinion does not give a specific reason. |

The definitions separated all eight examples. The strongest boundary is `supported_claim` versus `guide_or_solution`: classify by the post's main purpose. A verifiable explanation is `supported_claim`; actionable steps are `guide_or_solution`.
